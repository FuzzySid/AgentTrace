# Privacy review for captured content

Prompt and response text may contain personal information, customer records, credentials, or confidential material. Treat content capture as an explicit operational choice. Limit access, retention, and export destinations before enabling it.

Metadata can also be sensitive. Task IDs and retrieval query text may identify a person or reveal a business activity. Prefer stable pseudonymous identifiers and review attribute collection with the same care used for message content.
